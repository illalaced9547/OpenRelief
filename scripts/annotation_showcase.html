An annotation atlas built from real food-security histories and the exact explanations returned by our LLM annotation endpoint. Three countries. Three different outcomes. Every example traceable.

Training supervision, made visible. These are cached teacher annotations from the training partition. The teacher received the observed future IPC phase. They are not forecasts from the fine-tuned model, and generated mechanisms are hypotheses.

Selected to illustrate deterioration, persistent crisis and improvement; not a random sample of annotation quality. Rendering this gallery makes no API calls.
